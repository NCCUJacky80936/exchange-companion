# Destination research coverage

Research the user's actual journey. For every topic, prefer the government, consulate, school, municipality, transport authority, airline, insurer, or service provider responsible for the rule.

## Admission and academics

- nomination and application portals;
- acceptance deadline and required reply;
- programme and semester dates;
- course catalogue, credit system, Learning Agreement, enrolment, exam registration, transcript, and credit transfer;
- orientation, student card, campus accounts, buddy programme, accessibility, and student support.

## Immigration and money

- visa or entry permission based on nationality and stay length;
- residence registration and residence permit;
- required financial proof, accepted formats, current amount, currency, and validity;
- appointment, biometrics, fee, processing, and document rules;
- health insurance requirements for visa, enrolment, and the full stay.

## Housing and arrival

- student housing application, lease, deposit, payment, cancellation, key collection, and move-in inspection;
- airport or station to housing with luggage;
- local address registration, tax or identification numbers, bank needs, SIM, transit pass, utilities, broadcasting or municipal charges;
- public holidays, Sunday closures, waste, laundry, recycling, deposits, and common first-week needs.

## Safety and wellbeing

- emergency numbers, school and city contacts;
- medical care, prescriptions, vaccination or medication paperwork;
- mental-health and accessibility services;
- travel advisories and common scams from official sources.

## Packing

- actual airline allowances for every flight segment or ticket group, based on the current user's explicitly authorized e-ticket or itinerary rather than the demonstration journey;
- customs restrictions for food, medicine, cash, alcohol, tobacco, plants, and valuable equipment;
- battery, liquid, and carry-on restrictions;
- destination voltage, plug, climate, season, housing equipment, campus dress, and availability of local purchases;
- YouTube or lived-experience recommendations, clearly labelled as experience.
- begin with `config/packing-inspiration.json` when useful, while treating both included videos as item inspiration only and adapting findings to the current destination and season;
- accept user-pasted pending URLs from the latest authorized backup, then classify and verify them before proposing a resource.

## Return and travel

- deregistration, lease end, deposit, insurance, transport, SIM, bank, utilities, transcript, credit conversion, and return baggage;
- exchange-period trips, school/exam conflicts, visa-zone constraints, budgets, transport, accommodation, maps, notes, and trip packing.

## Freshness

Capture the verification date. Any deadline, price, legal rule, course list, airline allowance, visa procedure, or service plan should include a prompt to re-check close to use.
